---
title: "Liquidations"
description: "During the paper-trading alpha, we do not liquidate positions."
---

Liquidations are designed to prevent accounts from becoming undercollateralized and are based on overall portfolio risk, not individual positions.

Generally, liquidation is a protective move: we close positions (fully) against market liquidity. The system uses mark prices and available liquidity to avoid cascading failures. This protects you (from uncapped downside) and the market as a whole.

<Note>
  Liquidations do not currently occur during the paper trading alpha.
</Note>
