title: "Leaderboard"
description: "The leaderboard is public."
---

 It shows the top 10 traders by total PnL (realized + unrealized) for the timeframe you select.

### How it works:

- Timeframes: `Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `All-time`. If `Daily` is selected, the PnL shown is the change each trader generated over the past 24 hours.
- Data source: We take account snapshots every hour to preserve market integrity.
- Ranking cadence: Every 3 hours we compute PnL differences for the last day, week, and month (and update the leaderboard) using these snapshots.

<Expandable title="example">
  To produce the `Daily` view at 12:00, we compare the most recent snapshot (12:00) with the snapshot 24 hours earlier. The difference (realized + unrealized) is each trader’s `Daily` PnL. The updated leaderboard posts every three hours using the latest available snapshots.
</Expandable>

<Note>
  Small timing differences can occur between the live account view and the public leaderboard due to snapshot timing and the 3-hour publishing cadence.
</Note>
