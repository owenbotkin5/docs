---
title: "Paper Trading (Alpha)"
description: "Forum is currently in alpha testing."
---

We are hosting a “paper trading” environment during this period – no actual money is on the line. 

Upon account creation, each user is given **\$1,000 of virtual currency.** This currency cannot be exchanged for real money at any point in time. No user will ever be given additional virtual currency. In alpha, Forum does not involve the purchase or sale of real securities, commodities, cryptocurrencies, or other financial instruments.

<Note>
  No forced liquidations currently happen in this alpha.
</Note>
