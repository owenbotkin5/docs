title: "Market Making"
description: "Forum provides liquidity on the platform by making markets internally. "
---

Our automated system quotes bids and asks around the index value.

<Check>
  Our goal is to minimize funding by offering liquidity around the fair value of the market.
</Check>

At this stage, Forum does not utilize third-party market-making firms. All liquidity is provided internally. Our only goal is to provide deep liquidity across all markets to allow for adequate testing of our initial systems.

If you would like to make markets on Forum, you can get in touch at [<u>contact@forum.market</u>](mailto:contact@forum.market). We’ll be releasing a public API soon.
