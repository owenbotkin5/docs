---
title: "Market Making"
description: "Forum provides liquidity on the platform by making markets internally. "
---

Our automated system quotes bids and asks around a 24-hour time-weighted average value of the Attention Index. By using a 24-hour average rather than the instantaneous spot value, the market maker provides a buffer against temporary engagement spikes or "flash" cultural volatility, allowing for more orderly price discovery. 

<Check>
  The same 24-hour basis also aligns the market maker with our **funding-rate calculation**, minimizing funding payments.
</Check>

At this stage, Forum does not utilize external third-party market-making firms. All liquidity is provided internally. Our only goal is to provide deep liquidity across all markets to allow for adequate testing of our initial systems.

If you would like to make markets on Forum, you can get in touch at [<u>contact@forum.market</u>](mailto:contact@forum.market). We’ll be releasing a public API soon.
