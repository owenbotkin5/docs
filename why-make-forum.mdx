## The Attention Economy

Attention drives global value.

What people engage with online – via posts, comments, shares, views – affects real business decisions and outcomes. Some industries (often creative) depend entirely on engagement. Businesses and investors are thus left exposed to cultural risk (what’s popular, what’s forgotten).

Yet, today, there’s still no liquid, regulated way to manage that risk.

## A New Asset Class

By creating attention indices and listing futures on top, we create a new financial primitive, the first pricing layer for attention. 

**Forum measures both what the world currently cares about and what the world will care about.**

## Use Cases

Attention markets help businesses hedge cultural risk and help individuals profit from speculation.

### For Businesses

| Use Case                     | Description                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hedging marketing spend**  | Brands can offset uncertain ROI by shorting attention tied to a campaign, reducing downside if engagement underperforms.                          |
| **Competitive risk hedging** | Companies can hedge against competitors’ viral success by taking long positions in rival attention, softening the impact of lost mindshare.       |
| **Operational planning**     | Businesses exposed to local or indirect attention spikes (events, creators, launches) can hedge demand risk when budgeting inventory or staffing. |

### For Speculation

| Use Case                    | Description                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pure attention exposure** | Traders can take directional bets on future cultural relevance without relying on equity performance or company execution.                                                  |
| **Trading cultural trends** | Traders can profit from their ability to spot trends early: finding a rising artist before others catch on, or shorting an overly-popular trend before its attention drops. |

Attention markets even prove useful for passive observers. Forward-looking market prices, providing accurate information about future expetations for online engagement, allow businesses and individuals to make more informed, strategic decisions.
