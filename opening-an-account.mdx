---
title: "Opening an Account"
description: "Basic steps:"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title='Click "Get Started"'>
    This button is on the top right of our homepage and lets you create an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email">
    We use Privy for authentication. Upon entering your email, you will be prompted to enter a confirmation code that Privy sends to your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter confirmation code">
    Once confirmed, your account will be created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your username">
    This will appear on the public leaderboard, so pick a name you would be comfortable with others seeing.

    Usernames are not currently editable after-the-fact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage account">
    Once signed in, you can log out at any time by clicking the “Log Out” button at the top right of our homepage.
  </Step>
</Steps>
