title: "Overview"
description: "Perpetual-style futures are designed to give market participants synthetic long or short exposure to any underlying asset. "
---

That is, they allow market participants to trade an underlying asset without owning the asset itself. This occurs via a **funding rate mechanism**, which keeps the market price in-line with the underlying asset price.

<Info>
  In our case, the **Attention Index** serves as the underlying asset, or our “spot price.”
</Info>

Forum’s contracts are 5-year, cash-settled, perpetual-style futures. They trade continuously, are anchored to the underlying Attention Index, and are designed to give participants long or short exposure to changes in public attention. 

At the expiration date, the contract settles to the time-weighted average value of the Attention Index (converted 1:1 to USD) over the preceding 24 hours. Positions are fully cash-settled. No underlying media or IP changes hands.
